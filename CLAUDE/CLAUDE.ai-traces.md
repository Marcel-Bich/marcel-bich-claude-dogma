# AI Traces Rules

<rules>
1. **No curly quotes** - Use straight quotes " (U+0022), never “ ” (U+201C/U+201D) or ‘ ’ (U+2018/U+2019)
2. **No em-dashes** - Use - (U+002D) or --, never — (U+2014) or – (U+2013)
3. **No smart ellipsis** - Use three dots (...), never … (U+2026)
4. **No AI phrases** - Avoid "Let me...", "I'll...", "Sure!", "Certainly!", "Great question!", "I'd be happy to..."
5. **No emojis in code** - Not in source, comments, identifiers, or logs. Only in UI/user output.
</rules>

For details see `GUIDES/ai-traces.md`
